# GB-158 — Carry the settlement date through to postings
<!-- module: 7 · labels: story, agent-ready -->

**Type:** Story · **Priority:** Medium · **Epic:** Settlement alignment
**Repositories:** global-bank-account, global-bank-transaction · **Reporter:** Settlement Services

## Description

Settlement Services need two dates on a posting. The first is the date it takes economic effect:
`valueDate`, which we already carry. The second is the date the cash actually settles. For same-day
items the two are the same. For anything routed through the overnight cycle they differ, and today we
cannot tell them apart afterwards.

Add a `settlementDate` to the posting contract. Send it from the payroll disbursements in
global-bank-transaction, and store it on the posting.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A posting request may carry `settlementDate`. When it is missing, it defaults to `valueDate`.
2. The value is stored and returned by `GET /api/v1/postings/{id}`.
3. global-bank-transaction sends it for payroll batches.
4. **Callers that do not send the field keep working unchanged**, for at least one release.
5. Both repositories' tests pass at every step. No commit may leave main broken in either repository.
