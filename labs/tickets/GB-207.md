# GB-207 — Balance as at a date
<!-- module: 4, 6 · labels: story, agent-ready -->

**Type:** Story · **Priority:** High · **Epic:** {GB-100} Client money integrity
**Repository:** global-bank-account · **Reporter:** Payments Operations

## Description

Operations reconcile with the custodian at the close of each business day. The current balance
endpoint does not help them. It includes postings that have not yet taken economic effect.

They need the balance of an account **as at a given date**: the position the custodian would see on
that date.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `GET /api/v1/accounts/{accountId}/balance?asAt=YYYY-MM-DD` returns the balance as at that date.
2. **A posting that has not yet taken economic effect on that date is not included.**
3. The endpoint without `asAt` behaves exactly as it does today.
4. An unknown account is still rejected the way it is today.
5. Covered by tests.

## Notes

Operations were clear: this is about the date a posting *takes effect*. It is not the date we
received or recorded it. They have been caught by that difference before.

About half an hour.
