# GB-186 — A payroll batch must post all-or-nothing
<!-- module: capstone · labels: story, agent-ready -->

**Type:** Story · **Priority:** High · **Epic:** GB-100 Client money integrity
**Repositories:** global-bank-account, global-bank-transaction · **Reporter:** Client Money Ops

## Description

Payroll runs are paid as a batch, but we post them one instruction at a time. When one item fails
part-way through a run, the ledger holds some of the batch and not the rest.

Operations then work out by hand, from the ledger, which employees were paid and which were not.
Only then can they safely re-run anything. In March this took two people most of a day.

A payroll batch must land completely, or not at all.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A batch of postings can be sent in one request, and it is applied **atomically**. If any item is
   rejected, no posting from that batch exists in the ledger.
2. The response says **which** item was rejected and why. Operations can then fix it and re-send
   without reading the ledger.
3. A batch of up to **500** items is accepted. A larger batch is rejected with a client error (4xx).
4. global-bank-transaction sends each payroll run as one batch.
5. **Existing single-posting callers are unaffected**, for at least one release.
6. Both repositories' tests pass at every step of the merge sequence.
